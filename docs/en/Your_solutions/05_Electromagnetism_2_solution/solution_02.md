To find the magnetic field at the midpoint between two parallel wires, we use **Ampere’s Law** (specifically the derived formula for a long straight wire) and the **Right-Hand Rule**.

---

### 1. Identify the Given Information
*   **Current in Wire 1 ($I_1$):** $5\text{ A}$ (assume "up" the page)
*   **Current in Wire 2 ($I_2$):** $5\text{ A}$ (opposite direction, "down" the page)
*   **Distance between wires ($d$):** $10\text{ cm} = 0.1\text{ m}$
*   **Distance to midpoint ($r$):** $5\text{ cm} = 0.05\text{ m}$
*   **Permeability of free space ($\mu_0$):** $4\pi \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$

---

### 2. Determine the Direction (Right-Hand Rule)
To find the direction of the magnetic field ($B$) at the midpoint:
1.  **Wire 1 (Upward current):** Point your right thumb up. Your fingers curl **into** the page at the midpoint.
2.  **Wire 2 (Downward current):** Point your right thumb down. Since the midpoint is to the *left* of this wire, your fingers also curl **into** the page.

Since both fields point in the **same direction**, the total magnetic field ($B_{\text{total}}$) is the sum of the two individual fields.

---

### 3. Step-by-Step Calculation

**Step A: Formula for a long straight wire**
The magnetic field at a distance $r$ is given by:
$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$

**Step B: Calculate the field from Wire 1 ($B_1$)**
$$B_1 = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times 5}{2\pi \times 0.05}$$
$$B_1 = \frac{2 \times 10^{-7} \times 5}{0.05} = \frac{10 \times 10^{-7}}{0.05}$$
$$B_1 = 2 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$$

**Step C: Calculate the field from Wire 2 ($B_2$)**
Since the current ($5\text{ A}$) and distance ($0.05\text{ m}$) are identical to Wire 1:
$$B_2 = 2 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$$

**Step D: Calculate the Total Magnetic Field**
$$B_{\text{total}} = B_1 + B_2$$
$$B_{\text{total}} = 2 \times 10^{-5} + 2 \times 10^{-5} = 4 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$$

---

### 4. Final Result
*   **Magnitude:** $4 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}$ (or $40\text{ \mu T}$)
*   **Direction:** The field points **into the page** (perpendicular to the plane formed by the two wires).
