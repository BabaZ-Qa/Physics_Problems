## 5. Energy Stored in a Capacitor
**Problem:** A parallel-plate capacitor has the following parameters: $S = 0.02\text{ m}^2$, $d = 5\text{ mm}$, and $U = 500\text{ V}$. Calculate the capacitance ($C$), the stored energy ($W$), the electric field intensity ($E$), and the force of attraction ($F$) between the plates.

### Solution:
*   **Area ($S$):** $0.02\text{ m}^2$
*   **Distance ($d$):** $5\text{ mm} = 0.005\text{ m}$
*   **Voltage ($U$):** $500\text{ V}$
*   **Permittivity of Free Space ($\varepsilon_0$):** $\approx 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\text{ F/m}$

---

### Step 1: Calculate Capacitance ($C$)
**Formula:** $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \cdot S}{d}$
1. $C = \frac{8.854 \times 10^{-12} \cdot 0.02}{0.005}$
2. $C = 8.854 \times 10^{-12} \cdot 4$
3. **Result:** $C \approx 3.54 \times 10^{-11}\text{ F}$ (or $35.4\text{ pF}$)

---

### Step 2: Calculate Stored Energy ($W$)
**Formula:** $W = \frac{1}{2} C U^2$
1. $W = 0.5 \cdot (3.54 \times 10^{-11}) \cdot (500)^2$
2. $W = 0.5 \cdot 3.54 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 250,000$
3. $W = 1.77 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 250,000$
4. **Result:** $W \approx 4.425 \times 10^{-6}\text{ J}$ (or $4.43\text{ \mu J}$)

---

### Step 3: Calculate Electric Field Intensity ($E$)
**Formula:** $E = \frac{U}{d}$
1. $E = \frac{500}{0.005}$
2. **Result:** $E = 100,000\text{ V/m}$ (or $10^5\text{ V/m}$)

---

### Step 4: Calculate Force of Attraction ($F$)
**Formula:** $F = \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_0 S E^2$ (or $F = \frac{W}{d}$)
1. Using $F = \frac{W}{d}$:
2. $F = \frac{4.425 \times 10^{-6}}{0.005}$
3. **Result:** $F \approx 8.85 \times 10^{-4}\text{ N}$ (or $0.885\text{ mN}$)
