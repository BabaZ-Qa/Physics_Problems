## 7. Wavelength and Frequency
**Problem:** The human eye is most sensitive to light with a wavelength of about $550\text{ nm}$. Determine the color this corresponds to in the visible spectrum and calculate its frequency.

### Solution:
*   **Wavelength ($\lambda$):** $550\text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$
*   **Speed of Light ($c$):** $\approx 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$

---

### Step 1: Identify the Color
The visible spectrum ranges approximately from $400\text{ nm}$ (violet) to $700\text{ nm}$ (red). 
*   A wavelength of **$550\text{ nm}$** falls directly in the **green-yellow** part of the spectrum. This is specifically the peak sensitivity of the human eye's photopic (daytime) vision.

---

### Step 2: Calculate the Frequency ($f$)
**Formula:**
$$c = \lambda \cdot f \implies f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$$

**Steps:**
1. Substitute the known values:
   $$f = \frac{3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{550 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m}}$$
2. Simplify the calculation:
   $$f = \frac{3}{550} \times 10^{17}$$
   $$f \approx 0.00545 \times 10^{17}$$
3. Convert to standard scientific notation:
   $$f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$$

---

### Final Result:
*   **Color:** Green / Yellow-Green
*   **Frequency:** $5.45 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$ (or $545\text{ THz}$)
