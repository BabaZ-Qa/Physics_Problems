## 8. EM Spectrum
**Problem:** List the following types of electromagnetic radiation in order of **increasing wavelength**: Infrared, Ultraviolet, Microwaves, X-rays, Radio waves, Gamma rays.

### Solution:
To solve this, we must understand the inverse relationship between energy/frequency and wavelength. High-energy radiation (like Gamma rays) has the shortest wavelength, while low-energy radiation (like Radio waves) has the longest wavelength.

http://googleusercontent.com/image_content/174



---

### Step-by-Step Ordering (Shortest to Longest):

1.  **Gamma Rays:** Highest frequency and energy; shortest wavelength (smaller than an atom).
2.  **X-rays:** High energy; used in medical imaging; wavelength is roughly the size of an atom.
3.  **Ultraviolet (UV):** Shorter wavelength than visible light; carries enough energy to cause sunburns.
4.  **Infrared (IR):** Longer wavelength than visible light; felt as heat.
5.  **Microwaves:** Used in radar and cooking; wavelengths range from millimeters to centimeters.
6.  **Radio Waves:** Lowest frequency and energy; longest wavelengths (ranging from meters to kilometers).

---

### Final Result:
The order of **increasing wavelength** is:

$$\text{Gamma rays} < \text{X-rays} < \text{Ultraviolet} < \text{Infrared} < \text{Microwaves} < \text{Radio waves}$$
