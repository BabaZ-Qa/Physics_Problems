## 10. Speed of Light in Media
**Problem:** What is the speed of light in a diamond, which has an index of refraction $n = 2.42$?

### Solution:
*   **Index of Refraction ($n$):** $2.42$
*   **Speed of Light in Vacuum ($c$):** $\approx 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ (or $299,792,458\text{ m/s}$ for higher precision)

---

### Step 1: State the Formula
The index of refraction ($n$) of a material is defined as the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum ($c$) to the speed of light in that material ($v$):
$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$

To find the speed in the material, we rearrange the formula:
$$v = \frac{c}{n}$$

---

### Step 2: Step-by-Step Calculation

**A. Substitute the known values:**
$$v = \frac{3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{2.42}$$

**B. Perform the division:**
$$v \approx 1.23966 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$$

**C. Round to significant figures:**
$$v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$$

---

### Final Result:
The speed of light in a diamond is:
$$\mathbf{v \approx 1.24 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}$$

> **Physical Insight:** Light travels significantly slower in diamond than in air or vacuum. In fact, it travels at less than half its vacuum speed ($1/2.42 \approx 41\%$), which contributes to the high "sparkle" or dispersion effect seen in diamonds.
